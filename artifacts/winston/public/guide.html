Talking to Emma

Emma understands natural, conversational language — there are no commands to memorize. Just speak or type the way you'd talk to a close friend.

Voice or Text

Voice Mode

Tap the microphone button and speak. Emma listens, transcribes, and responds with her voice. Tap again to stop recording.

Text Mode

Type your message in the text box and hit Send — or press Enter. Emma responds in text, with voice if it's enabled.

Tip: You can mix voice and text freely. Emma keeps track of the conversation throughout your session, so she remembers what you talked about earlier.
⏰

Reminders & Push Notifications

Emma can set reminders that arrive as push notifications on your phone — and she'll speak them aloud at the right time.

Setting a Reminder

"Remind me to call Olivia at 3 PM."
"Don't let me forget to pick up dry cleaning tomorrow morning."
"Every day at 8 AM remind me to take my Meloxicam."
"Remind Susan she has a dentist appointment Thursday."

Recurring Reminders

Just say "every day", "every Monday", or "every weekday" and Emma sets it up as a recurring reminder automatically.

Viewing Upcoming Reminders

Tap the amber clock pill in the top-right corner of the app to see your upcoming reminders. You can cancel any of them from there.

Reminder vs. Calendar: "Remind me" sets a push notification. "Add to my calendar" creates a Google Calendar event. Emma knows the difference — but if you're not sure, just ask and she'll clarify.
📅

Google Calendar

Connect your Google account and Emma can read your calendar and add new events.

"What's on my calendar tomorrow?"
"Add lunch with Susan to my calendar Friday at noon."
"Schedule a haircut for Saturday at 10 AM."

Connecting Google

Tap the Connect Google button in the top bar to link your Google account. Once connected, a green Gmail badge appears — that means your calendar and contacts are active.

Morning Briefing

Every morning, ask Emma for your briefing and she'll run through everything relevant to your day — all in one natural conversation.

"Give me my morning briefing."
"Good morning — what's going on today?"
"What's on the agenda?"

What's Included

Weather

Current Dallas conditions and today's forecast. She'll mention Knoxville if Olivia has something going on.

Your Calendar

Today's appointments and anything important coming up this week.

Market Summary

SPY, DIA, and QQQ — a quick snapshot of how the market opened.

News

Top stories covering markets, global politics, and technology — the topics you care about.

Sports Scores

Rangers and Cowboys scores and standings when they're in season.

Medications

A gentle reminder about any medications due today.

Navigation

Ask Emma to take you somewhere and she'll open Google Maps with directions automatically.

"Navigate to the gym."
"Take me to Moody YMCA."
"Directions to Doctor Bonnet's office."
"Navigate to Louie's."

Emma knows your home, both YMCAs, your doctor's office, and your favorite restaurants. She'll also keep track of any places you ask her to save.

Open in Maps button: If a navigation message appears, look for the Open in Maps pill button in the message bubble — tap it any time to open Google Maps directly.

Lists & Tasks

Emma maintains running lists for you — shopping, to-dos, or anything you want to track.

"Add milk and eggs to my shopping list."
"What's on my shopping list?"
"Clear the shopping list."
"Add 'call the plumber' to my to-do list."

Susan's Lists

Emma can also help you track things Susan needs to do. Just say something like "remind Susan to call the vet" and Emma will save it and help you follow up.

Medications

Emma tracks your medications and sends reminders at the right times.

"Add my Meloxicam — I take it every morning."
"What medications am I taking?"
"Remove my statin from the list."

Medication reminders arrive as push notifications just like regular reminders, so you'll get a notification on your phone at the right time.

Memory Book for Olivia

One of the most meaningful things Emma does — she gently captures your stories and memories to save for Olivia. Every evening, she'll ask you one warm question. Everything you share gets saved to Olivia's memory book.

"Emma, I want to tell you a story about when Olivia was little."
"Save that one for Olivia's book."
"How many memories have I captured for Olivia?"
"Read me one of the stories I saved."

Emma keeps every story safe and will eventually help you compile them into a memory book for Olivia.

🌙

Evening Wind-Down

Each evening, Emma checks in with you — a gentle routine that asks how your day went, captures a memory for Olivia, and helps you unwind before bed.

"Good night, Emma."
"Start my evening wind-down."

Setting Your Wind-Down Time

Open Settings (gear icon in the top-right) and set the time you'd like your evening check-in. You'll get a push notification when it's time.

Restaurant Recommendations

Emma knows your taste — great dive bars, dark classic restaurants, strong drinks, and good food. Ask her anytime for a recommendation.

"Where should I take Susan for dinner tonight?"
"I'm in the mood for sushi — any suggestions?"
"Find me a good steakhouse near Preston Hollow."

When Emma recommends a restaurant, she'll offer to pull up their phone number or check OpenTable for availability right away.

⚙️

Settings

Tap the gear icon in the top-right corner to open Settings. From there you can:

  • Change Emma's voice — Choose from a selection of voices and preview each one before saving. Your current voice is Bex.
  • Change Emma's name — Rename your companion. Emma will greet you with her new name using her voice to confirm.
  • Upload a profile photo — Set a custom photo that appears in the header. Supports JPG, PNG, and WebP, up to 5 MB.
  • Evening wind-down time — Set the time Emma checks in with you each evening.
  • Push notifications — Enable or disable push notifications from the bell icon in the header.
