Welcome to Winston
Everything you need to know to get the most from your companion

Winston is a personal AI companion designed for people who want the benefits of modern AI without the complexity. Unlike Alexa, Siri, or Google Assistant, Winston knows who you are — it remembers your family, your schedule, your preferences, your goals, and the things you've told it matter to you.

You talk to Winston through a persona — either Rosie or MACC — who does the actual talking, listening, and remembering. Winston is the platform underneath; Rosie or MACC is who you're actually getting to know.

This guide walks through everything Winston can do. You don't need to read it all at once — start with Getting Started and explore the rest as you go.

🚀
Getting Started
From sign in to your first Morning Run Down

Getting started takes about 15 minutes.

  1. Sign in with Google. No password needed — just approve the sign-in and you're in.
  2. Choose your companion. Pick Rosie or MACC — each has a distinct personality and voice. You can change this later in Settings.
  3. Choose a voice. Listen to the available voice samples for your chosen persona and pick the one that feels right.
  4. Tell your companion about you. Through a warm conversation, your companion learns your name, your family, your schedule, your favorite places, and what you care about.
  5. Connect Google. Optionally connect Gmail and Google Calendar so your companion can summarize your emails and manage your schedule.
  6. Your first Morning Run Down. You'll get a real, personalized briefing right away, built from everything you just shared.
Tip: The more you tell your companion during onboarding, the better your first Morning Run Down will be. Don't rush — take your time.

The Stoic Philosophy Behind Winston
Why an ancient framework runs through a modern companion

About 2,300 years ago, a group of thinkers developed a philosophy built not for lecture halls, but for actually living — Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, Seneca. Their central question was simple: how do you live a good life in a world you can't control? Their answers turned out to be remarkably durable, not because the world stayed the same, but because people didn't.

Woven quietly through everything your companion does is one of their core practices: morning preparation, evening review. Marcus Aurelius did this every day — not to predict the future, but to meet it with intention instead of reaction, and then to look back honestly at how the day actually went. That's the same shape as your Morning Run Down and Evening Wind Down, every day, whether you notice it or not.

This isn't a lecture your companion delivers at you. The philosophy shows up as a real quote each morning — the actual words, then a plain, modern explanation of what it means — and it can quietly shape the angle of an evening reflection question when it genuinely fits. It's a long curriculum, not a one-off: each stretch of days centers on a different core idea, from the dichotomy of control (focusing only on what's actually yours to control) to amor fati — literally "love of fate," the idea that what's already happened is material to work with, not something to resent.

The goal isn't to make you a philosopher. It's the same thing Winston is built around more broadly: helping you live with a little more intention, one day at a time.

The Morning Run Down
Your personalized, freshly-built start to every day

Every morning, your companion delivers a real, freshly-generated briefing — just say good morning and they take it from there. Each one is put together live, so it takes a few moments; it's not an instant canned message.

Your Morning Run Down includes, in order:

📰
Headlines
Around ten real national and world stories, plus a couple tied to your own interests that rotate day to day.
🌤️
Weather
Today's current conditions and high/low, nothing more.
📈
Markets
Pre-market futures direction and overnight financial news ahead of the opening bell.
🏆
Sports
The final score from the most recent completed game for each team you follow.
✨
One Good Story
Something a little unexpected or delightful — never politics, crime, or celebrity gossip.
🏛️
Stoic Quote
A line from the Stoics, in the original words, then explained in plain modern language — never the same framing twice.
Note: Your calendar, to-dos, and email are not part of the Morning Run Down itself — check those directly with your companion any time, or through their own screens.

Evening Wind Down
A real check-in to close out your day

Each evening, your companion checks in with a real conversation, not a generic prompt:

📋
Day Recap
What happened, what got done, what's still hanging open.
🔭
Tomorrow
A brief look ahead, if there's anything on your calendar.

Then, one genuine reflection question — always different, meant to actually make you think: what didn't go as hoped and what you'd do differently, a moment worth being grateful for, a choice you're proud of or still unsure about.

If your companion has noticed something worth mentioning recently — a pattern, a connection to one of your goals — it may come up naturally as part of the conversation, woven into the recap or the question itself rather than announced separately.

The Attic
A place for anything that catches your attention

The Attic is a place to save anything that catches your attention — no folders, no organizing, nothing to file. If you're not sure where something belongs, it belongs in the Attic.

Try saying
Put this in the attic.
Rosie, remember this.
File this away.

You can also forward things by email: forward anything with "the attic" in the subject line to save it as-is, or forward anything with "recipe" in the subject line and your companion will pull out just the recipe — even from a messy webpage full of ads, or from a recipe you've simply pasted in as text.

What makes the Attic different from a notes app: your companion actively looks for connections between what you've saved — a pattern, something that relates to a goal, or a cluster of saves that might be the start of something bigger. When it notices something worth mentioning, it'll bring it up naturally, usually during your Evening Wind Down. You can review what's been noticed, browse everything saved, and clean out anything old right from the Attic screen in the menu.

My Goals
The pursuits that matter to you

My Goals is for the things you're pursuing — not chores, not resolutions, just the pursuits that matter to you: learning bridge, appreciating fine wine, getting into jazz.

Start a conversation from the My Goals screen and tell your companion what you're interested in. Rather than a wall of background information, it'll land on one real, concrete starting point — a specific track, book, class, or place to start — and explain why it fits you specifically. If it feels like a real goal worth keeping, your companion will ask if you want it saved; just say yes.

Three States

Active — something you're pursuing now.

Aspirational — real and worth keeping in mind, just not right now.

Completed — done.

Sometimes a goal starts itself — if your companion notices a real pattern in what you've been saving to the Attic, it may ask if you'd like to make it an actual goal.

Everything Else Winston Can Do
Your complete feature reference

🛒 Lists — Shopping, Wish List, Recipes, and More

Your companion keeps as many lists as you want — just name one and start saving to it. A new list is created automatically the first time you save something to it.

Try saying
Add milk, bread, and butter to my shopping list.
Save this recipe to my recipes list.
Save that paddle to my wish list.

If you don't name a list, groceries default to Shopping and bigger considered purchases default to Wish List. Recipes and detailed saves keep their full content, not just a title. And when your companion gives you a good recommendation, just say "save that" afterward and it saves the real thing — not a guess at what it said.

🔔 Reminders & To-Dos

Try saying
Remind me to call my sister at 3pm.
Remind me to take my medication every morning at 8am.
What reminders do I have today?

Your companion tells the difference between a reminder ("remind me to...") and a real calendar appointment ("add to my calendar...") — if it's not sure which you mean, it'll ask.

🚗 Departure Alerts

For anything on your calendar with a real location, your companion calculates actual drive time from home using live traffic conditions and lets you know exactly when you need to leave — not a fixed buffer, the real time for that day's traffic.

📁 My Records

Your companion automatically scans your email for confirmations worth keeping — trips, warranties, home services, subscriptions, vehicle registrations, and more — and organizes them for you. Nothing needs to be forwarded or set up; it happens on its own. Each record keeps the vendor, confirmation number, dates, address, phone, website, and amount — tap in to view, edit, or remove anything.

📦 My Orders

Orders you place online are picked up from your email automatically and tracked in one place — retailer, item, carrier, current status, and expected delivery date, with a full tracking timeline for each.

💊 Medications

A private, PIN-protected space to track your medications — name, dose, frequency, and reminder times, with reminders included in your Morning Run Down. Your companion also checks for potential interactions and flags anything worth knowing about. This is for awareness only — always consult your doctor or pharmacist before making any changes. You can export or share your full medication list anytime.

🗺️ Navigation

Try saying
Take me to my doctor.
Directions to the gym.

Winston opens Google Maps with your destination already entered.

Tip: Add new places anytime — "Add my hairdresser at 123 Main Street to my saved places."

📅 Calendar, Email, Texting & Calls

Calendar — read, create, modify, or delete events through natural conversation.

Email — your companion reads your inbox, flags anything suspicious, and can act on emails directly: trash, archive, mark read, or draft a reply for your approval.

Texting — your companion can draft a text, but can never send it on your own behalf — it hands off a pre-filled message to your Messages app for you to send.

Calls — ask your companion to place a call to anyone in your contacts.

Reservations — ask for a restaurant reservation and your companion handles the details.

📍 Things Worth Doing Nearby

Twice a week — Monday and Thursday — your companion sends a short notification with a few genuinely curated things worth checking out nearby, personalized to you. Tap the notification to see the full details and talk through any of them.

💰 Bills

Your companion tracks bills that require manual payment (not autopay) — name, due date, and amount — and includes them in your Morning Run Down as they come due.

My People & Winston Connect
The people who matter, and staying in touch through Winston

My People is where you keep the people who matter — name, relationship, phone, email, address, birthdays, anniversaries, and anything else your companion should know about them. Add someone manually, or pull them straight from your Google Contacts.

Tip: Add "emergency contact" to someone's notes and they'll automatically show up on the Emergency screen.

Winston Connect is for people who also use Winston. Invite anyone from your list — share an invite link, or enter their Winston username directly if you know it. Once they accept, you're connected, and you can send real messages or reminders straight through Winston — it delivers them, and you'll see the full back-and-forth as an actual conversation thread. Disconnect anytime; you can always reconnect with a new invite.

You can also create Groups to coordinate with several connected people at once.

Frequently Asked Questions
Honest answers to common questions
Does Winston sell my data or show me ads?
Never. Winston is a subscription service ($9.50/month) specifically so it never needs to monetize your data. Your information is used exclusively to make Winston more helpful to you.
What's the difference between Winston and Alexa or Siri?
Alexa and Siri answer questions but know nothing personal about you. Winston knows your name, your family, your goals, your routines, and what you've told it matters to you — and it remembers.
Can Winston access my bank account or financial data?
No. Winston only tracks bills and reminders you tell it about directly, and never connects to any financial institution.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Your data is always exportable. We retain data for 30 days after cancellation before permanent deletion.
Can I use Winston on multiple devices?
Yes — everything syncs automatically. Add a reminder on one device and it shows up everywhere.
Is Winston a medical service?
No. Winston is not a medical device or healthcare provider — including the medication interaction checker, which is for awareness only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. In a genuine emergency, always call 911 directly.
How do I get help if something isn't working?
Just ask your companion directly — "What can you do?" or "How do I set a reminder?" — or reach out to us directly for support.
