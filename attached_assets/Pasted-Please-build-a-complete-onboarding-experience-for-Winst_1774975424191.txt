Please build a complete onboarding experience for Winston. This is the most important feature in the entire product so please build it carefully and beautifully. Here are the requirements. When a new user opens Winston for the first time they should experience a warm conversational onboarding flow where Emma Peel introduces herself and gets to know the user through natural conversation — never a form or questionnaire. The onboarding should follow this exact script and flow.
Scene 1 — Welcome: Emma Peel speaks first unprompted saying Hello I've been looking forward to meeting you. I'm going to be your personal companion here every morning when you wake up and whenever you need me throughout your day. Before we get started I'd love to get to know you a little. Is that alright? User responds affirmatively. Emma Peel says Wonderful. Let's start with the most important thing — what's your name?
Scene 2 — Name and Location: Emma Peel learns the user's name and uses it warmly throughout. Asks where they live and what time they wake up. Confirms she'll be ready for them at that time.
Scene 3 — The People: Emma Peel asks who matters most in their life. Follows up warmly on each person mentioned. Asks where they live so she can track weather for them. Continues until user feels done.
Scene 4 — Health and Wellbeing: Emma Peel gently asks about medications, recurring appointments, anything health related she should know. Frames it around care not data collection. Makes it optional and comfortable.
Scene 5 — Your Places: Emma Peel asks about places the user goes regularly — doctor, gym, favorite spots, anywhere they go often. Confirms each one and saves it.
Scene 6 — What You Love: Emma Peel asks about shows, music, sports teams, restaurants, hobbies. Responds with genuine warmth and curiosity to each thing shared. This should feel like the most natural and enjoyable part of the conversation.
Scene 7 — Voice Selection: Emma Peel offers to play voice samples so the user can choose the voice that feels most comfortable. Plays four options — warm British female, warm American female, calm American male, friendly British male. Saves the user's choice as their permanent companion voice.
Scene 8 — Story Archive Introduction: Emma Peel explains that every evening she'll ask one question about their life and memories to build a beautiful record of their story for the people they love. Asks if they'd like that.
Scene 9 — First Briefing: Emma Peel says Alright [name] I think I know enough to get started. Let me tell you about your day. Then immediately delivers a personalized morning briefing using everything just learned including weather at their location, their schedule if any, and a warm personal closing.
Technical requirements. Store everything learned during onboarding in Supabase as the user's permanent profile. Use this profile to populate Emma Peel's system prompt for all future conversations. Detect when a user is new versus returning — new users see onboarding, returning users go straight to the main chat interface. The entire onboarding should use Emma Peel's voice throughout — fully conversational with voice input and voice output. Show a subtle progress indication so users know roughly where they are in the process without it feeling clinical. Make the interface warm and minimal during onboarding — just Emma Peel's name, a soft waveform animation when she's speaking, and the user's text input at the bottom.
