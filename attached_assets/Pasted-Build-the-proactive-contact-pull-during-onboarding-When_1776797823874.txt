Backend — contacts.ts: fetchFamilySuggestions()

Fetches up to 200 of the user's Google Contacts (sorted by most-recently-modified, so family is usually near the top) and applies three heuristics in priority order:

Explicit relation labels — contacts where Google Contacts knows the relationship (spouse, daughter, father, sibling, etc.). These come back marked high confidence with the exact label ("Wife", "Son", "Grandmother", etc.)
"Family" contact group — contacts the user has manually placed in Google's built-in Family group. Also high confidence.
Same last name — contacts sharing the user's last name (extracted from their profile). Marked medium confidence, labeled "Family".
Returns up to 8 suggestions sorted: high-confidence first, then by whether they have a phone number. Gracefully returns an empty array if the user has no Google auth or hasn't granted contacts scope.

Backend — onboarding.ts: GET /api/onboarding/suggested-people

Session-authenticated endpoint. Gets the user's full name from their profile (for the last-name heuristic), calls fetchFamilySuggestions, and returns the results. Falls back to { suggestions: [] } on any error — Scene 5 never breaks even if contacts can't be fetched.

Frontend — Onboarding.tsx: Scene 5 suggestion cards

When the user enters Scene 5, the endpoint is called once in the background. If suggestions come back, a "From your Google Contacts" panel appears above the chat input — a compact scrollable list of cards. Each card has:

Letter avatar → turns to a green check when confirmed
Name + relationship badge (e.g., Daughter, Spouse, Family)
✎ Pencil — makes the relationship label inline-editable (type a correction, press Enter to confirm or Escape to cancel)
✓ Green check — adds the person to collectedData.people immediately, card goes green
✗ X — dismisses the card without adding
Skip all link in the header to clear everything at once
Confirmed people flow silently into the onboarding data — Emma's Scene 5 conversation picks them up naturally, and they get saved to the profile at completion. Users who signed in without Google auth, or whose token lacks contacts scope, simply see no cards and the step works exactly as before.
