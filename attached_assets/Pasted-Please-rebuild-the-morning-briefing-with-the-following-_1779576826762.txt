Morning Briefing — New Structure
The briefing should flow in this exact order:

Weather — One sentence only, actionable. Tell the user what they need to know and what to do about it. Example: 'It's going to be 94 and humid with storms rolling in around 3pm — might want to move that afternoon run to the morning.' Never just list temperature and conditions.
Calendar — What's on today. Flag any conflicts. If a meeting has related email context, mention it. Example: 'You have a call with John at 10am — he emailed yesterday about the Henderson project, might be worth a quick review before.'
To-Do List — Scan the user's to-do list. Surface anything due today or undated. Ask naturally if they want to tackle any of them or schedule them. Never just list them robotically.
Email Scan — Only what matters. Meeting requests, follow-ups, anything urgent. Skip routine emails entirely.
News — Top 3-5 stories people will be talking about today. 2-3 sentences each. Factual only, no commentary or opinion.
One Funny Story — Something light the user can repeat later in the day. Man bites dog style.
Local Events — What's happening nearby that might interest this user based on their profile. Brief description and a link to more information. New restaurants worth knowing about.

The Stoic Moment
After the briefing, shift into the Stoic moment. This is not random — it follows a progressive 365-day curriculum stored in the database.
The format is:

A real quote from Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, or Seneca. Attributed correctly. Exact words.
One sentence from Winston connecting it specifically to something in the user's actual day — a meeting, a challenge, a goal, something from their calendar or to-do list.
An invitation to reflect: 'Take a moment with this today. If something comes to you, just tell me and I'll save it to your journal.'

The Stoic Curriculum
Add a new table stoic_curriculum with columns: id, day_number (1-365), quote, author, source, theme, phase (1-6).
Populate it with the 365 quotes organized into 6 phases:

Phase 1 (Days 1-60): Foundation — What Is In Your Control
Phase 2 (Days 61-120): Perception — How You See Determines What You Feel
Phase 3 (Days 121-200): Action — The Discipline of Doing
Phase 4 (Days 201-270): Will — Amor Fati, Loving What Is
Phase 5 (Days 271-330): Virtue and Legacy
Phase 6 (Days 331-365): The View From Above

Add a column stoic_day to user_settings to track which day of the curriculum each user is on. Increment by 1 each morning. After day 365, reset to 1.
The first 7 days of the curriculum should introduce the Stoics gently before the main curriculum begins — brief context on who Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, and Seneca were, woven naturally into the briefing.
Onboarding Mention
If the user has not completed onboarding, mention it once naturally at the end of the briefing: 'By the way, I notice we have not finished getting me fully set up yet. Would you like to schedule some time today to complete your profile so I can do a better job for you?' Only once per day maximum. Never repeat it.
Configuration
Each section of the briefing should be individually toggleable via user settings. Store preferences in user_settings as boolean columns: briefing_weather, briefing_calendar, briefing_todos, briefing_email, briefing_news, briefing_funny, briefing_events, briefing_stoic. All default to true.
