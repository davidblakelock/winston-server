3. Winston Connect — Full Group Feature
Build a complete group coordination system on top of the existing Winston Connect infrastructure:
Groups:

New Supabase tables: connect_groups (id, name, created_by, created_at) and connect_group_members (id, group_id, user_name, added_at)
POST /api/connect/groups/create — creates a named group with initial members
GET /api/connect/groups — returns all groups the user belongs to with member list
POST /api/connect/groups/:id/add-member — adds a Winston Connect user to a group
DELETE /api/connect/groups/:id/remove-member — removes a member from a group
DELETE /api/connect/groups/:id — deletes a group (creator only)
PUT /api/connect/groups/:id/rename — renames a group

Group Messaging:

POST /api/connect/groups/:id/message — sends a message to all group members via Winston Connect
POST /api/connect/groups/:id/reminder — sends a reminder to all group members

Group Scheduling:

POST /api/connect/groups/:id/find-time — queries Google Calendar availability for all group members who are Winston users, returns mutually available time slots in a specified date range
POST /api/connect/groups/:id/propose-time — sends a time proposal to all group members
POST /api/connect/groups/:id/confirm-time — confirms a proposed time and adds to all participants' calendars who are Winston users, sends text to non-Winston members

Chat Integration:
